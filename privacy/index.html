Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Cat Chess does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

No accounts, ads or analytics

The game has no sign-in, shows no advertising and contains no analytics or tracking code.

Your progress stays on your device

Settings, saved games, statistics and puzzle progress are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are removed if you uninstall the app.

Local network (LAN) play

If you choose to play on your local network, the game connects directly to another device on the same network to exchange chess moves. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party, and the game does not use the internet for this.

Exported games

If you use “Export PGN”, the game file is saved on your device and copied to your clipboard; what you do with it is up to you.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional purchases or ads), this page will be updated before that version is released.
