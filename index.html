Cat Chess

Chess, but everyone is a cat.

Kittens are your pawns, knights wear tiny helmets, rooks hide in cardboard boxes, and when a piece is captured the attacker stalks in and pounces.

Become a playtester Coming soon to Google Play

Cute, but proper chess

Six cat opponents

From Kitten, who chases yarn instead of checkmates, to Sabertooth: ancient, patient and merciless. Every level is genuinely stronger than the last.

30 cat puzzles

Mate in 1, 2 and 3. Every puzzle is computer-checked to have exactly one winning first move.

Clocks and blitz

10, 15 and 30 minute games, plus 3+2 and 5+3 blitz when you're feeling quick on the paw.

Play a friend

Two players on one phone, or two phones on the same Wi-Fi with a simple invite code.

Every rule

Castling, en passant, promotion, stalemate, threefold repetition and the fifty-move rule. Plus hints, undo and draw offers.

Make it yours

Five breeds, four boards, a music-box soundtrack, stats and 17 achievements to collect.

The Cat Wardrobe

Dress every piece for the grandest game of chess.
